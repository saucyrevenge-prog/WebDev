Website and service terms

Last updated: October 17, 2026

These terms explain how this website, its offers and its estimator are used. Services are provided by Christian Soucy in Fredericton, New Brunswick. Project-specific terms are confirmed in an accepted written quote or service agreement.

Estimates are not orders

The estimator provides a planning estimate in Canadian dollars for the basic scopes described beside its options. Selecting features, viewing a total or submitting an inquiry does not place an order, reserve a start date, authorize recurring payments or create a bill.

The final quote confirms requirements, shared work between features, any additional complexity, applicable taxes, external costs and the delivery schedule. Existing client agreements are not replaced by a new website price or estimator update.

The $500 bilingual website offer

The offer covers up to five pages, each in English and Canadian French, an email quote request form and one revision round, using established layouts. Five bilingual pages means up to five English versions and five matching French versions. Fewer than five pages keep the same base price.

Extra bilingual pages are $150 CAD each. A revision round means one consolidated set of requested changes within the agreed scope. Extra revisions, new pages, booking, portals, payments, custom design and other integrations are separate work. The estimator shows starting prices for basic versions; the accepted quote defines the deliverables.

Domains and outside costs

Domain registration and renewals are separate from the website build, hosting and care. The domain price is confirmed separately before purchase or renewal arrangements are made. A domain is not included merely because hosting or care is selected.

Payment processing fees, third-party subscriptions, licences and usage charges are separate where applicable. Any required outside services and who is responsible for their accounts and charges are identified in the project quote.

Hosting and ongoing care

Hosting and care are optional monthly services, shown separately from the build. Basic hosting starts at $25 CAD/month and does not include feature maintenance. Care starts at $99 CAD/month for the basic scope, with additions for extra pages, custom design and supported features as shown in the estimator.

Care includes hosting, availability and selected basic-feature checks, and up to 30 minutes of routine content updates per month. New functionality and larger changes require a separate quote. Hosting is included in care and is not charged a second time as a separate hosting plan.

The agreed monthly scope and price are confirmed before service begins. Later changes to features, scope or recurring charges are confirmed with you under your agreement; changing an estimator selection does not change an existing bill.

Project approvals, payment and cancellation

The accepted quote or service agreement sets out payment dates, any deposit, milestones, delivery, revisions, renewal and cancellation arrangements before work begins. Recurring billing requires an agreed arrangement and any necessary payment authorization. This website does not collect card details or take payments through its inquiry form.

Scope changes and their effects on price or timing are agreed before the additional work starts. Any cancellation, refund, suspension or handover terms must be specified in the accepted agreement, subject to rights that cannot be excluded by law.

Content, access and ownership

You must have permission to supply the text, images, logos, data and account access needed for the project. Do not send credentials through the public inquiry form. The project agreement confirms ownership or licensing of deliverables, third-party components, account responsibilities and handover arrangements. Existing third-party licences continue to apply.

Portfolio examples describe selected work and may link to external sites. Their current availability and features can change, and some require an account.

Using this website

Use the site lawfully. Do not misuse the form, submit malicious content, attempt unauthorized access or interfere with other visitors. I aim to keep descriptions and prices accurate; errors can be corrected before an accepted quote is issued. No particular search ranking, business result or uninterrupted service is promised by the estimator.

These website terms do not override mandatory legal rights or an existing accepted client agreement. Changes to this page apply going forward and do not retroactively change agreed project terms.
