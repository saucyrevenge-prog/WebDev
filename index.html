05 / About the developer

ONE PERSON.

I’m Christian Soucy, an independent developer based in Fredericton, New Brunswick.

Building and operating service businesses has shaped how I approach software. I care about the details that only become obvious when people actually have to use a system: understandable navigation, sensible permissions, a booking that doesn't confirm itself, and tools that do what they're supposed to.

My work spans design, frontend development, backend systems, integrations, deployment and ongoing support. The goal isn't just to ship a website. It's to make something useful and keep it useful.

Frequently asked

GOOD QUESTIONS.

Things worth knowing before we get into the details.

Do you build websites for small businesses?+

Yes. Tailored websites for local service companies, trades and businesses that need more than a generic template.

Can you improve an existing website?+

Absolutely. Sometimes a redesign is best; other times, fixing navigation, speed, forms or fragile features makes more sense.

Can you build booking systems and customer portals?+

Yes. I've worked on approval-based bookings, availability windows, customer accounts, service histories and staff-facing workflows.

Will you maintain my site after launch?+

Yes. Ongoing support can include hosting coordination, updates, fixes, deployments and new features.

How much does a project cost?+

It depends on the scope. We'll review the requirements and agree on a clear estimate before development starts.
