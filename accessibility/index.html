Accessibility

Last updated: October 17, 2026

I want visitors to be able to read this site, use the estimator and contact me in English or French. If something gets in your way, please let me know so I can help and work on the problem.

Features included in the site

  • Skip-to-content links, structured headings and labelled navigation.
  • Keyboard-operable links, buttons, forms and estimator choices, with visible focus styles.
  • Labels for form controls and status announcements for inquiry feedback and estimate changes.
  • Separate English and Canadian French pages with language information and a language switcher.
  • Responsive layouts and reduced animation when your device requests reduced motion.

These features describe implementation work, not a guarantee that every page works with every browser or assistive technology.

How I approach accessibility

I use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, as a reference for ongoing improvements. Full conformance has not been formally assessed, and this statement is not a certification or a claim that every criterion has been met.

Current limitations and alternatives

Live price calculations, saved estimator choices and saved language preferences require JavaScript and, for saved data, browser storage. Static service descriptions and prices remain readable without the calculator. Separate English and French page addresses are available.

The inquiry form requires JavaScript for submission. If the form or estimator is difficult to use, email saucyrevenge@gmail.com with what you need. I can discuss the same information by email.

Linked client websites, hosted payment services and other third-party pages have their own interfaces and accessibility practices. The moving banner pauses on hover or keyboard focus and respects the reduced-motion setting.

Report a barrier

Email Christian Soucy at saucyrevenge@gmail.com. Include the page address, what you were trying to do and what prevented you from doing it. Browser, device or assistive-technology details can help, but you do not need to disclose a diagnosis or other sensitive information.

You can also ask for information from this site in another usable format. I will review the request and discuss a practical way to provide it.

Ongoing review

This statement will be updated as features are changed and barriers are identified or addressed. Accessibility feedback helps guide that work.
