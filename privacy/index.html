Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy covers soucy.company, its pricing estimator and its inquiry form. Christian Soucy, based in Fredericton, New Brunswick, is responsible for the personal information handled through this site.

Information you choose to share

The inquiry form asks for your name, email address, requested service, optional budget and project message. If you bring an estimate into the form, the message includes your selected pages, features, monthly plan and any project notes. I use this information to reply, prepare a quote and discuss the services you requested.

Submitting an inquiry does not add you to a marketing list. I do not sell your personal information. Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, identification documents or other sensitive records through this public form.

Using the estimator without sending an inquiry

You can use the estimator without entering contact details. Its calculations run in your browser. Your selected options and draft project notes are saved in the current tab’s session storage so you can switch languages and prepare an inquiry. The draft is not sent to me unless you submit the inquiry form.

Page counts, feature choices and plan selections can also appear in the page address when you switch languages or open the inquiry. Anyone you share that address with may see those choices. Your typed project notes are not added to the address by the estimator.

Browser storage and cookies

The site saves your English or French preference in local browser storage until it is cleared. It saves the estimator draft in session storage for the tab’s browser session; a browser’s session-restoration settings can affect how long that draft remains available.

The site does not use advertising pixels or behavioural advertising. Hosting and security providers may process operational request information or use technologies needed to deliver and protect the site. You can clear this site’s saved language preference and estimator draft below, or manage website data in your browser.

Using the language selector or estimator again may save new preferences. This control does not delete inquiry emails or your browser history.

Retention and security

Inquiry and service correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the client relationship and meet applicable business or legal record-keeping needs. Ask me about a particular record if you want to know whether it is still held or can be deleted.

The site uses HTTPS and form validation to help protect submissions. No website or email system can guarantee complete security. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

Your choices and privacy questions

You can request access to or correction of your personal information, ask for deletion, or withdraw consent for future uses, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping requirements. I may need to verify your identity. Withdrawing information needed to respond or provide a service may limit what I can do for you.

Contact Christian Soucy at saucyrevenge@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. You can also learn about privacy rights or raise a concern with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes

The date on this page identifies the latest update. Material changes to how personal information is used will be explained, and further consent will be sought where required. This policy does not replace the privacy terms of a separate client application or service agreement.
